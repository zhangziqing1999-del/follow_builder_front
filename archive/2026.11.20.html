agentagentsworkflow
Aaron Levie
ceo @box - your business lives
Regardless of what it’s called, we’re clearly moving to yet another era of AI agents in our work. We’ve quickly jumped from agents that spit out a quick answer for you, to today agents that you orchestrate inside of a set of tasks. But as agents can both fan out and work in parallel, handle much larger context, be able to do long running work, and have far greater levels of capabilities, we’re entering yet another level of what we can have AI do for us. We’ll soon be able to give agents a set of goals, and they can go out and design the best solution for that goal, while also continuously improving it based on the ongoing execution of whatever it built. This will obviously start with engineering because that’s where we’re seeing the best practices emerge first in almost every new era of agents. But the impact can be quite massive across the rest of knowledge work. You’ll tell an agent to improve your client onboarding workflow to achieve a certain targeted goal; or have agents go reduce risk in your business; or improve your security posture on an ongoing basis. These autonomous systems will be able to take on a set of goals, design the best outcome, test and iterate, deploy, and then optimize whatever it built on an ongoing basis. Importantly, this leads to outcomes and results that humans literally couldn’t have accomplished before. This will lead to fairly new levels of productivity with AI.

tokentokensinference
Guillermo Rauch
@vercel CEO
I’m being pitched a new inference neocloud company on average every two days. No exaggeration. I take every entrepreneur seriously and I look deeply into each. But it’s hard to fathom this being sustainable. At the same time, AI Gateway token demand on Vercel has been ~40% MoM compounded since the beginning of the year. No signs of stopping. One way to reconcile this is that compute is the new economy, and tokens the new energy unit. There’s a new Industrial Revolution taking place. Every aspect of our lives is being replatformed on top of this new system that enhances everything. More like an “oil rush” than a “gold rush”: it’s durable, not a fad. In the short term, I anticipate more expansion, followed by consolidation due to economies of scale, cybersecurity and enterprise requirements (ZDR and cyber being a hot topic for inference providers), capital requirements, and an evolving regulatory landscape.
