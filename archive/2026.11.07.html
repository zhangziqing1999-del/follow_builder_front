agentagentscontext
Aaron Levie
ceo @box - your business lives
We’re going to have 100X more agents in the enterprise working with our data and systems than even people do today. Every AI agent deployment comes down to how successfully they can get access to the critical context to make a decision, do research, or execute tasks. 90% of the data in an enterprise is unstructured data, which means this critical context is living inside of our documents, contracts, marketing assets, research materials, financial plans, design files, and more. Which means we need effective ways for agents to get access to this critical enterprise content to other work. We’re excited to partner with NVIDIA to deliver advanced context retrieval -combining lexical and semantic search in Box- to ensure that AI agents can find the most relevant information they’re looking for. All in a way that adheres to their enterprise security, access controls, and data governance policies, so agents only work with the data they’re allowed to. Great to work with @NVIDIAAI and @JensenHuang on this partnership. Files 🤝 GPUs.
