This is a huge opportunity right now for those starting these firms. I wouldn’t count out existing players that commit to the transformation either as existing customer trust goes a long way. 12 tips for people who posted on LinkedIn but now wanna post on X A lot of people I know are moving their posting from LinkedIn to X. When they ask me how I grew 80k followers on X in one year, here’s what I tell them: 1. LinkedIn is a stage. X is a party. First you find your crowd, then you listen to what they're already talking about. Don't start talking until you get it 2. You can't reach the C-suite directly. LinkedIn is a professional network, so you can go straight to decision makers. X doesn't work like that. You have to get mass attention first, and then the right people notice you 3. Post way more than you think. On LinkedIn, 3-5 posts a week counts as a lot. I posted 3 times a day on X for 6 months. Some founders I follow post 5 times a day. It's not that they're on X all day. They just don't overthink it 4. Make posting a reflex. If you spend too long on a post, you won't post. Whenever a thought comes up, post it right there. No planning, no scheduling, no waiting for the right time 5. It's a numbers game. Most posts won't do well. Maybe 1 in 10 will. Maybe 1 in 50 goes viral. That's normal 6. Half your posts should be reactions. LinkedIn is mostly “proactive content”. On X, at least at the start, half of what you post should be “reactive content”: replies, quote tweets, commentary on what others already said 7. Know the difference between a reply and a quote tweet. A reply is like a LinkedIn DM. It says "notice me." A quote tweet uses someone else's content to grow your own account 8. Fix your feed first. If you wanna talk about cybersecurity and your feed is all politics, you can't join the right conversations. Pick a few big names in your niche. See who they follow and who they engage with, and follow those people too. Like, bookmark and comment. After about a week your timeline will look totally different 9. Write like you're texting an acquaintance. Polish turns people off. Everyone's allergic to perfect AI-sounding writing. And please don't use AI or bots to write your replies. People can tell, and they might block you 10. Post the stuff that's obvious to you. Make a list of the top 10 questions customers, investors and your team ask you. That's where you're the expert. What seems too obvious to talk about is exactly what people want to hear 11. Post what you just said out loud. If you say something in real life and people go "huh, never thought of it that way," post it on your way back. Don't wait, you'll forget. You don't have to block out time to think of what to say. Just say what you've already said 12. Show your face, and don't sell yet. X is a wall of text, so video stands out. I just record myself on my laptop with something on the screen. People wanna know you're a real human, not AI slop. And until you have about 1,000 followers, don't promote your company. Share real opinions first. Later, when you repost company news, always add your own take, not PR speak. Remember: people follow people, not companies
