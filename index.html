modelmodels
Aditya Agarwal
General Partner @SPCCo-Founder @Bevel_HealthEx: Early Eng @facebook
There are basically two kinds of companies to build right now. 1. Diffusion of AI This is the "vertical SaaS" of today. There are a bunch of different models for it (FDE company, rollup, horizontal product) but ultimately it all boils down to the same thing: get AI diffused very close to where the work is being done. Profound, Cognition, Harvey etc. A lot of great companies will get built here. 2. Frontier of AI This is where the really interesting action is. Use AI to accelerate scientific progress, then build products from that progress. It is a harder category to see. But we are seeing a lot more of it at @spc: - Externa is adding hundreds of new chemical building blocks to proteins, so you can engineer a drug to match the disease instead of settling for what nature gave you. - Epana Bio is using genomics and AI to design autoimmune therapies that go after the cellular origin of the disease. - VenomicsAI is doing AI-accelerated drug discovery from venoms. - Equivariant is doing multi-physics modeling for the semiconductor industry. (There are always picks-and-shovels for AI as well. Lots of amazing companies in that category.) Category 2 is not well understood or much talked about externally. It should be because I think that is where the vast majority of model driven returns will come from.
