My top 5 takeaways from @nateparrott on how to get Claude to design without AI slop: 1. Give Claude context upfront. Share screenshots, pages you like, and your past work. For an existing product, have Claude rebuild the real screens from code. 2. Research familiar UX patterns. For hard layout problems, ask Claude to research familiar products that solve something similar and note the layouts people already know how to use. 3. Make Claude aware of its design biases. "The model has no idea what its own biases are." In your design.md or another file, consider discouraging Claude's tells like serif text, off-white backgrounds, and card-edge bars. 4. Start with wireframes. Have Claude create wireframes first and give it feedback to nail your core user flows before going deep on fonts and colors. 5. Ask for multiple options, then remix them. Ask Claude for 5 options for key screens with different fonts, colors, and styles. Then pick the parts you like and ask it to remix and create 5 more options. 📌 Watch the full episode now: https://t.co/HPX5HjXafX Also available as a written post: https://t.co/jcofmkQc01
